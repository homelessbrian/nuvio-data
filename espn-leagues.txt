# Every built-in league (from the app's own SportsCatalog.kt) that goes straight to ESPN's plain
# scoreboard endpoint, read by scripts/espn.py every 5 minutes into the shared espn/*.json cache.
#
# Format per line: <app league id>|<ESPN path>|<extra query string, or blank>
# These three mirror exactly what SportsCatalog.kt already has for the league (its id, its
# espnPath, and its espnQuery when it has one for college sports' &groups= filter) — this file
# was generated from that source once; keep the two in step by hand when a league is added,
# removed, or renamed there, the same way leagues.txt is kept in step with TheSportsDB by hand.
#
# Leagues ESPN routes through something other than its plain scoreboard — HockeyTech (the AHL,
# ECHL, PWHL, OHL, WHL, QMJHL, USHL), the MLB Stats API (Minor League Baseball), or ESPN's own
# cricket score panel (the IPL, Big Bash, SA20, Major League Cricket, international cricket) —
# are left out on purpose. The app's own SportsMatcher.kt (OtherFeeds) fetches those directly;
# duplicating their fetch logic here would be a second place for it to drift out of step.
